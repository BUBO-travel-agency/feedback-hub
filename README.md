## One place to start. Private spaces to collaborate.

This is the public information page for our feedback workflow. Feedback itself is reviewed in private GitHub Projects shared with invited collaborators.

| Report a problem | Suggest an improvement | Refine our content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Describe what happened and what you expected. | Explain the idea and who it would help. | Point to the exact page and describe the correction. |

## For reviewers

1. **Accept your GitHub invitation.** Use the GitHub account you shared with your BUBO coordinator.
2. **Open your workspace.** Your coordinator will send you the link to the private project you are invited to.
3. **Review the feedback.** Follow the project's instructions and the access level assigned to you.

Access to this repository and access to a private project are managed separately. A repository invitation alone does not open a private workspace.

## Send feedback

Use the feedback form provided on the relevant BUBO website or in BUBO Admin when it is available to your account. Include the affected page, a short title and a clear description.

For workspace access or questions about a submission, contact your BUBO coordinator through your usual channel.

> **Keep feedback private.** This repository is public. Do not publish customer details, internal screenshots, credentials or operational information here. Public Issues are not used for collecting feedback.
